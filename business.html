About this business

Tata Main Hospital is Jamshedpur's premier multi-specialty medical facility, established in 1917. It serves as the primary healthcare provider for the region with over 850 beds and more than 40 specialized departments including Cardiology, Oncology, Neurology, Orthopedics, and Pediatrics.


The hospital offers 24/7 emergency services, a dedicated ICU, advanced diagnostic imaging, and a blood bank. It is widely recognized for its patient-centric approach and modern medical infrastructure.
